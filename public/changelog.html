Changelog

Everything new on the site since it launched, newest first

A whole number, like 3.0, means a big change to the site. A decimal, like 3.1, means smaller additions and fixes.

  1. 4.2 3 Nov 2026

    Cleaner player and tournament records

    • 45 players who were listed twice, under two separate entries, are now one player. Their whole career and every head-to-head record now show in one place.
    • When two results sites report the same tournament differently, the smaller leftover page no longer names a champion. It points you to the full event instead, for example Göteborg Open 2016 and EM 2022 Open.
    • This changelog, so you can see what's new.
  2. 4.1 3 Nov 2026

    Fixes and polish

    • About 5,200 games that two results sites both reported were counted twice. Each one now counts once, so records and ratings are more accurate.
    • Some names with letters like ö and å showed up as a jumble of symbols. They now read properly.
    • Tournaments that share a name, like the 127 events called “SM”, now show their year.
    • Players who share a name show a city, birth year or other detail in the search, so you can tell them apart.
    • The home page no longer jumps around while it loads, and player pages start loading sooner.
    • Fixes on phones: the year filter no longer opens off the edge of the screen, the menu button fits on small phones, chart labels no longer overlap, and Records has no empty gaps.
    • A matchup with no games now links to both players' pages instead of a dead end.
    • Going back to a player or matchup you looked at earlier no longer leaves the rating chart and the forecast missing.
    • The logo and Head-to-head links on the ratings and tournament pages take you to the home page.
    • Easier to use with a keyboard or a screen reader, calmer for people who turn off animations, and the numbers in the scoreline grid are easier to read in both light and dark mode.
    • The site now always uses a secure connection.
  3. 4.0 2 Nov 2026 Major update

    Ratings and forecasts

    Every player now has a rating, and matchups get a forecast.

    • Every player gets a rating worked out from every game they've played, where a big win counts a little more than a narrow one. Player pages show the rating over their whole career, with their peak.
    • Matchup pages show If they met today: each player's chance to win, draw or lose a game played now, from their ratings and how they've done against each other.
    • With Playoff and Series picked, the forecast shows each player's chance of winning a best-of-7 series.
    • A new page, How the ratings work, explains the ratings and the forecast, and how well they would have predicted past results.
    • Against each nation on player pages shows the countries a player does better or worse against than their rating says.
    • Above and below the ratings shows the opponents a player beats more, or less, often than expected.
    • A story rail on player and matchup pages sums up each section as you scroll: who leads, when the lead changed, the most common score and more.
    • A new share image, sized for social media feeds, with both ratings, the forecast and recent form. You can preview it, pick light or dark, and share, copy or download it.
    • After you pick a player, their career page is the first choice in the list, so it's easier to find.
  4. 3.3 1 Nov 2026

    Tournament pages and daily updates

    • Every tournament has its own page with the champion and runner-up, the biggest upsets, the playoff bracket round by round, and the group standings.
    • Tap a tournament in a list of games to open its page.
    • Results now update every day instead of once a week.
    • Links you share now show the players' names and record in the preview in chat apps and on social media.
    • The browser tab shows which player or matchup you're looking at.
    • Text appears faster, because the fonts now load from the site itself.
    • The site is now checked automatically for screen reader support and readable contrast, and what those checks found has been fixed.
  5. 3.2 30 Oct 2026

    A new home at h2h.puck.no

    • The site moved to h2h.puck.no. Old links still work and take you there.
    • The crest of Norges Bordhockeyforbund, the Norwegian table hockey federation, is the new site logo.
    • Results from table-hockey.eu were added as a third source, so more games are included.
    • Records is in the top menu, and the footer lists where the results come from.
    • Better at spotting the same game reported by more than one site, so it only counts once.
  6. 3.1 29 Oct 2026

    Records and the story behind each game

    • A Records section on the home page: longest winning streak, longest unbeaten run, perfect record, most wins in a row over one rival, the most even rivalry and the longest wait for a rematch. Each record has its own graphic.
    • Open a game in the list to see what it meant: the playoff series it was part of, the head-to-head record after it, the previous meeting and the tournament level.
    • Tap a player's name on a matchup page to swap in a different opponent without starting over.
    • Players and Rivalries in the top menu now take you to the top-ranked players and the biggest rivalries.
  7. 3.0 24 Oct 2026 Major update

    A whole new look

    The site was redesigned from top to bottom, with much more to explore on every page.

    • Home: find a player straight away, and see the most-played rivalry and the biggest rivalries between the world's top-ranked players.
    • Matchups: a scoreboard with a one-line verdict, the last 10 games, a chart of who led over time, results tournament by tournament, a side-by-side tale of the tape, the most common scores and a season-by-season view.
    • Players: key numbers at the top, win rate by season, who they play most, and a table of every opponent with a button to compare.
    • A new top menu with search, a menu on phones, and the “/” key to jump to search.
    • Your browser's Back button takes you to the matchup you saw before.
    • Easier to read, and names in Cyrillic letters display properly.
  8. 2.0 14 Oct 2026 Major update

    Design refresh

    A cleaner, calmer site that puts the result first.

    • The result of a matchup is shown once, in a big scoreboard with a player on each side.
    • Stages and filters are together in one bar under the scoreboard, with the number of games in each stage.
    • A new home screen with the headline numbers and the top-ranked rivalries.
    • A new game strip with one bar for every game: up for one player's wins, down for the other's, and taller for bigger wins.
    • A score-first list of games on phones, and a score bar that stays at the top as you scroll.
    • A new logo and app icon, so the site looks right when you add it to your home screen.
    • The same date and number style everywhere.
  9. 1.5 6 Sep 2026

    Better for screen readers

    • Screen readers now read out a short summary of each matchup's result.
  10. 1.4 1 Aug 2026

    Smarter player search

    • A player listed under more than one name or spelling shows up once in the search.
    • The search is easier to use with a keyboard.
  11. 1.3 18 Jul 2026

    Year filter and a fresh coat of paint

    • Filter games to a range of years.
    • The site shows how many players and games it covers.
    • Cleaner buttons, menus and game details.
    • A livelier animation when a player is on a run of seven or more wins.
    • Links keep the players and filters you picked more reliably.
  12. 1.2 21 Jun 2026

    Group stages sorted correctly

    • Games from team events and group stages now show under Round-robin, with clearer stage names.
  13. 1.1 12 Jun 2026

    Look up one player

    • Pick a single player to see every opponent they've faced, in a table you can sort and filter.
    • Shared links show a title and picture in chat apps and on social media.
    • A sharper, tidier share image.
  14. 1.0 11 Jun 2026 Launch

    Table Hockey H2H goes live

    • Pick any two players and see their full head-to-head record: wins, draws, losses and goals.
    • Every game they've played, with the tournament, the stage and the score, and a link to the tournament.
    • Look at round-robin and playoff games separately, and see playoff games as series.
    • Recent form, and charts of the rivalry and the goals over the years.
    • Make a share image or copy a link to any matchup.
    • Your recent matchups, one tap away.
    • Light and dark mode.
