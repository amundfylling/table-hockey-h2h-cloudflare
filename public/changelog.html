Head-to-head/Changelog

Changelog

Everything new on the site since it launched, newest first

A whole number, like 3.0, means a big change to the site. A decimal, like 3.1, means smaller additions and fixes. Each change is marked New for something you couldn't do before, Improved for something that works better, or Fixed for something that was wrong.

  1. 4.4 4 Nov 2026

    A guide to the site

    • New A guide walks you through the site with annotated screenshots: finding players, reading a head-to-head, player pages, the leaderboard, tournaments and records. It is linked from the bottom of every page.
  2. 4.3 3 Nov 2026

    Leaderboard and tournaments pages

    • New Results from the Czech federation (stiga.trefik.cz, Czech tournaments since 2000) and from Latvia (galdahokejs.lv and escore.live, 2023 to 2026) are now included, so Czech and Latvian players have far more games.
    • New A ratings leaderboard ranks every player by rating. You can filter it by nation, switch between active and all-time players, and sort by the last 90 days to see who is climbing. A slider sets a minimum number of games. It also shows each player's world rank next to their place by rating, so you can see who the world ranking under- or overrates.
    • New A tournaments page lists every event, newest first, with its winner and runner-up. You can search it and filter by year, country and level.
  3. 4.2 3 Nov 2026

    Cleaner player and tournament records

    • New This changelog, so you can see what's new.
    • Improved The formulas on How the ratings work are now typeset as proper maths, with fractions, square roots and sums, instead of plain text.
    • Fixed 45 players who were listed twice, under two separate entries, are now one player. Their whole career and every head-to-head record now show in one place.
    • Fixed When two results sites report the same tournament differently, the smaller leftover page no longer names a champion. It points you to the full event instead, for example Göteborg Open 2016 and EM 2022 Open.
  4. 4.1 3 Nov 2026

    Fixes and polish

    • Improved Tournaments that share a name, like the 127 events called “SM”, now show their year.
    • Improved Players who share a name show a city, birth year or other detail in the search, so you can tell them apart.
    • Improved Player pages start loading sooner.
    • Improved A matchup with no games now links to both players' pages instead of a dead end.
    • Improved Easier to use with a keyboard or a screen reader, calmer for people who turn off animations, and the numbers in the scoreline grid are easier to read in both light and dark mode.
    • Improved The site now always uses a secure connection.
    • Fixed About 5,200 games that two results sites both reported were counted twice. Each one now counts once, so records and ratings are more accurate.
    • Fixed Some names with letters like ö and å showed up as a jumble of symbols. They now read properly.
    • Fixed The home page no longer jumps around while it loads.
    • Fixed On phones, the year filter no longer opens off the edge of the screen, the menu button fits on small phones, chart labels no longer overlap, and Records has no empty gaps.
    • Fixed Going back to a player or matchup you looked at earlier no longer leaves the rating chart and the forecast missing.
    • Fixed The logo and Head-to-head links on the ratings and tournament pages take you to the home page.
  5. 4.0 2 Nov 2026 Major update

    Ratings and forecasts

    Every player now has a rating, and matchups get a forecast.

    • New Every player gets a rating worked out from every game they've played, where a big win counts a little more than a narrow one. Player pages show the rating over their whole career, with their peak.
    • New Matchup pages show If they met today: each player's chance to win, draw or lose a game played now, from their ratings and how they've done against each other.
    • New With Playoff and Series picked, the forecast shows each player's chance of winning a best-of-7 series.
    • New A new page, How the ratings work, explains the ratings and the forecast, and how well they would have predicted past results.
    • New Against each nation on player pages shows the countries a player does better or worse against than their rating says.
    • New Above and below the ratings shows the opponents a player beats more, or less, often than expected.
    • New A story rail on player and matchup pages sums up each section as you scroll: who leads, when the lead changed, the most common score and more.
    • Improved The share image was redesigned for social media feeds, with both ratings, the forecast and recent form. You can preview it, pick light or dark, and share, copy or download it.
    • Improved After you pick a player, their career page is the first choice in the list, so it's easier to find.
  6. 3.3 1 Nov 2026

    Tournament pages and daily updates

    • New Every tournament has its own page with the champion and runner-up, the biggest upsets, the playoff bracket round by round, and the group standings.
    • New Tap a tournament in a list of games to open its page.
    • Improved Results now update every day instead of once a week.
    • Improved Links you share now show the players' names and record in the preview in chat apps and on social media.
    • Improved The browser tab shows which player or matchup you're looking at.
    • Improved Text appears faster, because the fonts now load from the site itself.
    • Fixed New automatic checks for screen reader support and readable contrast found some problems, and they are fixed.
  7. 3.2 30 Oct 2026

    A new home at h2h.puck.no

    • New The site moved to h2h.puck.no. Old links still work and take you there.
    • New The crest of Norges Bordhockeyforbund, the Norwegian table hockey federation, is the new site logo.
    • New Results from table-hockey.eu were added as a third source, so more games are included.
    • Improved Records is in the top menu, and the footer lists where the results come from.
    • Fixed Better at spotting the same game reported by more than one site, so it only counts once.
  8. 3.1 29 Oct 2026

    Records and the story behind each game

    • New A Records section on the home page: longest winning streak, longest unbeaten run, perfect record, most wins in a row over one rival, the most even rivalry and the longest wait for a rematch. Each record has its own graphic.
    • New Open a game in the list to see what it meant: the playoff series it was part of, the head-to-head record after it, the previous meeting and the tournament level.
    • New Tap a player's name on a matchup page to swap in a different opponent without starting over.
    • Fixed Players and Rivalries in the top menu now take you to the top-ranked players and the biggest rivalries. Before, they went nowhere.
  9. 3.0 24 Oct 2026 Major update

    A whole new look

    The site was redesigned from top to bottom, with much more to explore on every page.

    • New Home: find a player straight away, and see the most-played rivalry and the biggest rivalries between the world's top-ranked players.
    • New Matchups: a scoreboard with a one-line verdict, the last 10 games, a chart of who led over time, results tournament by tournament, a side-by-side tale of the tape, the most common scores and a season-by-season view.
    • New Players: key numbers at the top, win rate by season, who they play most, and a table of every opponent with a button to compare.
    • New A new top menu with search, a menu on phones, and the “/” key to jump to search.
    • Improved Easier to read, with stronger contrast.
    • Fixed Your browser's Back button takes you to the matchup you saw before.
    • Fixed Names in Cyrillic letters display properly.
  10. 2.0 14 Oct 2026 Major update

    Design refresh

    A cleaner, calmer site that puts the result first.

    • New A new home screen with the headline numbers and the top-ranked rivalries.
    • New A new game strip with one bar for every game: up for one player's wins, down for the other's, and taller for bigger wins.
    • New A new logo and app icon, so the site looks right when you add it to your home screen.
    • Improved The result of a matchup is shown once, in a big scoreboard with a player on each side.
    • Improved Stages and filters are together in one bar under the scoreboard, with the number of games in each stage.
    • Improved A score-first list of games on phones, and a score bar that stays at the top as you scroll.
    • Improved The same date and number style everywhere.
  11. 1.5 6 Sep 2026

    Better for screen readers

    • Improved Screen readers now read out a short summary of each matchup's result.
  12. 1.4 1 Aug 2026

    Smarter player search

    • Improved A player listed under more than one name or spelling shows up once in the search.
    • Improved The search is easier to use with a keyboard.
  13. 1.3 18 Jul 2026

    Year filter and a fresh coat of paint

    • New Filter games to a range of years.
    • New The site shows how many players and games it covers.
    • Improved Cleaner buttons, menus and game details.
    • Improved A livelier animation when a player is on a run of seven or more wins.
    • Fixed Links keep the players and filters you picked more reliably.
  14. 1.2 21 Jun 2026

    Group stages sorted correctly

    • Fixed Games from team events and group stages now show under Round-robin, with clearer stage names.
  15. 1.1 12 Jun 2026

    Look up one player

    • New Pick a single player to see every opponent they've faced, in a table you can sort and filter.
    • New Shared links show a title and picture in chat apps and on social media.
    • Improved A sharper, tidier share image.
  16. 1.0 11 Jun 2026 Launch

    Table Hockey H2H goes live

    • New Pick any two players and see their full head-to-head record: wins, draws, losses and goals.
    • New Every game they've played, with the tournament, the stage and the score, and a link to the tournament.
    • New Look at round-robin and playoff games separately, and see playoff games as series.
    • New Recent form, and charts of the rivalry and the goals over the years.
    • New Make a share image or copy a link to any matchup.
    • New Your recent matchups, one tap away.
    • New Light and dark mode.
